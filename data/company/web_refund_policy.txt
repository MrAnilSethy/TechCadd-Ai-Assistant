Refund Policy
1. Refund Eligibility
a. Online Courses / Training Programs
● Refunds are available within 7 days of enrollment, provided that:
○ You provide a valid reason for your dissatisfaction.
● No refunds will be issued after 7 days of enrollment.
b. Software / Digital Products
● Due to the nature of digital products, no refunds will be issued once access/download has been granted.
● If you experience technical issues, we will provide assistance to resolve them.
c. Services (Consultation / Custom Projects)
● Refunds for services are only issued if the project has not yet begun.
● If a project has started, partial refunds may be considered based on the work completed.
2. Refund Process
To request a refund, please email us at info@techcadd.com with the following:
● Full name
● Registration number
● Service or product purchased
● Reason for refund
Our team will review your request and respond within 5 business days.
3. Exceptions
Refunds will not be granted in cases of:
● Change of mind after the refund window
● Failure to use/access the service or product
● Violation of our terms of use or code of conduct

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at:
info@techcadd.com
