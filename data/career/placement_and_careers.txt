TechCADD Placement and Careers

TechCADD states that it offers 100% placement support, along with internships and certifications, to help students build a strong career path. Placement information: https://techcadd.com/placement-cell/

Job openings are posted at: https://techcadd.com/jobs-public.php

Training programs useful for students and freshers: 6 Weeks, 45 Days and 6 Months Industrial Training, plus internship-style training in PHP, Django, SEO and React.

The website says courses are job-oriented, with hands-on projects and real-world scenarios aimed at employability in IT companies. Students in the testimonials mention becoming freelance designers, full stack developers, digital marketers and data professionals.
