The key types of data analytics include:-

Diagnostic Analytics:
Investigates data to find the causes of specific outcomes.
Predictive Analytics:
Leverages historical data to estimate future trends and behaviors.
Prescriptive Analytics:
Offers recommendations for actions based on data insights to meet specific objectives.
