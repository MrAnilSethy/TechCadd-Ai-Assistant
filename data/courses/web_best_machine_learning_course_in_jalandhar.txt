Question2:- What are the main categories of Machine Learning?
Machine Learning encompasses three primary categories: :-
Supervised Learning:
Involves training a model on labeled datasets to predict outcomes based on input features.
Unsupervised Learning:
Focuses on discovering hidden structures in unlabeled data, facilitating insights without predefined categories.
Reinforcement Learning:
Teaches models to make decisions through rewards and penalties, often used in robotics and game AI.
