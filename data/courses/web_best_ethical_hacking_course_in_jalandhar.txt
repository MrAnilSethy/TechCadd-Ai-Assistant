Question1:-What is Ethical Hacking?How does it differ from Malicious Hacking?
Ethical hacking is the authorized practice of probing systems for security flaws to enhance protection, setting it apart from malicious hacking, which exploits vulnerabilities for personal gain. Conducted with permission, ethical hacking aims to improve security. Those interested in pursuing this field can benefit from the
best
ethical hacking course in Jalandhar
, which provides essential skills for becoming a certified ethical hacker.
Question2:-What tools do ethical hackers use?
Common tools include Nmap for network scanning, Metasploit for penetration testing, Wireshark for traffic analysis, and Burp Suite for web application testing.
Question3:-Is it legal?
Yes, ethical hacking is legal when performed with the system owner’s permission, often outlined in a formal agreement to ensure compliance
Question4:- What ethical principles do hackers follow?
Ethical hackers adhere to principles such as integrity, legality, and confidentiality, ensuring they operate within defined boundaries and respect the rights of individuals and organizations. These principles are vital for those taking the
