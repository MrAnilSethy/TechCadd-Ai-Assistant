Web development involves crafting websites from scratch or enhancing existing ones, including everything from design to functionality and user interactions.

Question4:- Can you explain front-end vs. back-end?
Front-end development creates the visual and interactive aspects of a website, while back-end development focuses on the server, databases, and application logic that operate behind the scenes.
