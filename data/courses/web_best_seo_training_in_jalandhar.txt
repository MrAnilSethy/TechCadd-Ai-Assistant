Question1:- What is SEO?
SEO, or Search Engine Optimization, is the practice of fine-tuning your website to rank higher in search results, attracting more visitors without paying for ads.
Question2:-When can I expect to see SEO results?
SEO is a marathon, not a sprint; expect to see initial results in about 3 to 6 months, influenced by your niche and strategy.
Question3:- What are keywords?
Keywords are the search terms that users input into search engines. Picking the right ones helps you connect with your ideal audience.
Question4:-On-page vs. off-page SEO: what’s the difference?
On-page SEO involves optimizing your site’s internal elements—like content and layout—while off-page SEO builds your site’s credibility through backlinks and social signals.
