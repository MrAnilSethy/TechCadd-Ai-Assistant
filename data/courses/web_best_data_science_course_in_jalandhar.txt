Question1:- What is Data Science?
Data Science is an interdisciplinary field that blends statistics, mathematics, programming, and domain expertise to extract insights from structured and unstructured data. It encompasses data collection, cleaning, analysis, and visualization to support decision-making and drive business strategies. For aspiring professionals, the

Question2:-What skills are essential for a career in Data Science?
A successful data scientist should possess a blend of skills, including programming languages such as Python and R, statistical analysis, machine learning, and data visualization. Proficiency in tools like SQL and Excel is also essential. Moreover, strong problem-solving abilities and effective communication skills are crucial for interpreting data and conveying findings to stakeholders. Question3:- How is Data Science different from Data Analytics?
While both Data Science and Data Analytics focus on data interpretation, Data Science encompasses a broader range of techniques, including predictive modeling and machine learning. Data Analytics typically involves analyzing historical data to uncover trends and insights, whereas Data Science may also include creating algorithms and systems for future predictions.
